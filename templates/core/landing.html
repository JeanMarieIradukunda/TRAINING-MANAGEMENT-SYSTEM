What you can generate

Three documents, one consistent standard

Every document is built from the Centre's own sector, trade and module records, so what you generate always reflects the curriculum as it is currently configured.

Scheme of Work

A term-by-term breakdown of a module's learning outcomes, indicative content, learning activities, resources and formative assessment evidence.

Lesson Plan

Assessment Plan

How it works

From sign-in to a finished document, in four steps

The same process applies whichever of the three documents you're producing.

01

Sign in

Sign in with your administrator or trainer account. Trainer accounts are created by an administrator.

02

Choose trade, level & module

Select the sector, trade, level and module you are planning training for, then the relevant learning outcome(s).

03

Generate the document

Choose Scheme of Work, Lesson Plan or Assessment Plan. The system drafts the content from your curriculum data for you to review.

04

Review, edit & print

Adjust any field as needed, then print or export the finished document for your records or submission.

Access & generation policy

Who can sign in, and how generation access works

The three generators are only available to signed-in accounts — there is no anonymous access.

Account types

  • Administrators manage the full curriculum — sectors, trades, levels, modules, trainers and generation access — and can generate all three documents without restriction.
  • Trainers sign in with an account issued by an administrator and generate documents for the modules assigned to them.
  • Don't have an account yet? Contact your institution's administrator to have one created.

Generation allowance

  • Every trainer account includes one free generation, shared across the Scheme of Work, Lesson Plan and Assessment Plan generators.
  • After the free generation is used, continued access requires paid access, activated by an administrator once payment is confirmed.
  • Paid access remains active for a fixed period once granted, and can be renewed at any time by an administrator.
Payment instructions and renewal are handled directly by your administrator after your free generation has been used.
