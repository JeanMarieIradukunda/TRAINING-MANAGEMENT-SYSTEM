02Documents

Three documents, one consistent standard

Every document is drafted from the Centre's own sector, trade and module records, so what you generate always reflects the curriculum as it is currently configured.

A

Scheme of Work

A term-by-term breakdown of a module's learning outcomes, indicative content, learning activities, resources and formative assessment evidence.

Open
B

Lesson Plan

A complete, classroom-ready session plan for a single learning outcome — objectives, introduction, development steps, reflection and assessment.

Open
C

Assessment Plan

One row per learning outcome, covering assessment type, required resources and a practical note on administering it — ready for scheduling.

Open
03Process

From sign-in to a finished document, in four steps

The same process applies whichever of the three documents you're producing.

01

Sign in

Sign in with your administrator or trainer account. Trainer accounts are created by an administrator.

02

Choose trade, level & module

Select the sector, trade, level and module you're planning training for, then the relevant learning outcome(s).

03

Generate the document

Choose Scheme of Work, Lesson Plan or Assessment Plan. The system drafts the content from your curriculum data for you to review.

04

Review, edit & print

Adjust any field as needed, then print or export the finished document for your records or submission.

04Access

Who can sign in, and how generation access works

The three generators are only available to signed-in accounts — there is no anonymous access.

Account types

Administrator
Manages the full curriculum — sectors, trades, levels, modules, trainers and generation access — and generates all three documents without restriction.
Trainer
Signs in with an account issued by an administrator, and generates documents for the modules assigned to them.
No account yet
Contact your institution's administrator to have one created.

Generation allowance

First generation
Free, and shared across the Scheme of Work, Lesson Plan and Assessment Plan generators.
After that
Continued access requires paid access, activated by an administrator once payment is confirmed.
Once granted
Paid access stays active for a fixed period, and can be renewed at any time by an administrator.
Payment instructions and renewal are handled directly by your administrator after your free generation has been used.
